Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

On your device

Images are generated entirely on your iPhone. Your prompts, the photos you choose as references and the images you create are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to any AI service. Deleting the App deletes them.

Photos and camera

The App uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photos you choose, and uses the camera only when you take a reference photo. Either is used only as a reference for a generation you start. When you save an image, the App asks for permission to add it to your library; it cannot read your library. Nothing is uploaded.

Face data

A photo you choose as a reference may show a face. This is how the App treats it:

  • What is collected: only the photo you pick or take yourself. The App does not detect, scan, measure or map faces, does not use Face ID, TrueDepth or ARKit face tracking, and does not perform face recognition or identification. It never creates a faceprint, facial template or any other biometric identifier.
  • How it is used: solely as the starting image for the generation you request, processed by the image model on your iPhone. It is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, analytics or to train any AI model.
  • Sharing: never. The photo does not leave your device and is not sent to us, to any AI provider or to any other third party.
  • Storage and retention: a private copy is kept in the App's own storage on your device only while the generation that uses it is pending, and is deleted automatically as soon as that generation finishes or is cancelled. A photo you pick but never use is removed the next time the App starts.
  • Consent: you give consent by choosing the photo yourself, and you can withdraw it at any time by cancelling the generation. Only use photos of people who have agreed to it.

The images you generate stay on your device until you delete them in the App.

Model downloads

Models are downloaded from Hugging Face. As with any download, Hugging Face receives your IP address and basic request information under its own privacy policy. No prompts or images are sent.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Limitless Pro; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, not your name, email or payment details.

What we do not do

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Your choices

You can delete any image in the App, or delete the App to remove all of its data. For purchase-related data held by RevenueCat, or any other privacy request, contact supportappleap@gmail.com.
